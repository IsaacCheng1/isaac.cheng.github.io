Reinforcement Learning

It focuses on sequential decision making problems, by observing, taking action, observing, taking action, …

It learns from its own experience. It is more like intelligence.

RL is used in generative models and post-training.

How to represent experience as data ?

  • st - state at time t
  • ot - observation at time t
  • at - action/decision taken at time t
  • πt - trajectory, a sequence of states/observations and actions
    • often referred to as rollout or episode
  • r(s, a) - reward of s, a

Markov Property

The state s3 is independent of its previous state s1, given state s2. This helps us to break down the problem in terms of that we do not need to worry about all the dependencies that come before it.
